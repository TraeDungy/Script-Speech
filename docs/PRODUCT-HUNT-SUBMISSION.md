**Current situation:**

Podcast producers schedule recording sessions weeks in advance.  
eBook authors wait days for audio narration.  
Small businesses can't afford professional voiceovers.  

**Why it's painful:**
- Cost: Professional voice actors = $500-2000 per hour
- Time: Booking, recording, revisions = 2-4 weeks
- Flexibility: Can't quickly create variations
- Accessibility: Out of reach for bootstrapped creators

**Current solutions are broken:**
- Free TTS sounds robotic (bad for brand)
- Cheap TTS has limited voice selection
- Expensive agencies have long lead times
- Hiring freelancers requires ongoing management

**We fixed this.**

### Solution (300 words)

**Script-Speech is the easiest way to create professional voiceovers.**

How it works:
1. Paste your script (or upload text file)
2. Choose a voice (50+ AI voices, multiple languages)
3. Click "Generate"
4. Download MP3 (instant)

That's it. No voice actors. No scheduling. No waiting.

**Why creators love it:**

- **Speed:** From script to voiceover in 30 seconds

- **Quality:** Professional-grade AI voices
- **Flexibility:** Generate unlimited variations
- **Accessibility:** No technical skills required

**But existing tools suck:**
- Google Cloud TTS: $16-24 per 1M characters (expensive)
